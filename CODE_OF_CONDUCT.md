# Community Code of Conduct

OpenJev welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Technical disagreement is expected; personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully.
- Make room for questions and explain assumptions.
- Credit others' work and represent experiments honestly.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy and the confidentiality of reports.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate misrepresentation of results, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces managed by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, restrict participation, or block participants. Responses should reflect the severity and persistence of the behavior.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer through a private contact method they explicitly list on their hosting profile. The project has not yet published a dedicated conduct-reporting address. If no private channel is available, open an issue titled “Request for a private conduct contact” without incident details or personal information, and ask a maintainer to establish one.

If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer when available, or use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting process. Maintainers should keep reports private to the extent practical and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
